✅ Review Criteria

What makes a strong proposal

  • Clear problem statement with evidence
  • Realistic and itemised budget estimate
  • Measurable community benefits
  • Specific location with GPS or map reference
  • Identified number of direct beneficiaries
  • Community endorsement or support evidence
  • Feasible implementation timeline
  • Alignment with county development goals

💡 Tips for Success

Improve your proposal quality

📍

Be Specific

Include exact location, GPS coordinates if possible, and specific community names.

👥

Show Community Need

Attach a support letter signed by community elders, group leaders, or ward rep.

💰

Justify Your Budget

Break down estimated costs clearly. Vague budgets reduce proposal credibility.

📸

Attach Visuals

Photos of the site or affected area strengthen the case for the project.
